# Agency Facts (Love Productions)

The verified facts about Love Productions itself. These are the **only** facts
allowed in an agency credibility post (`agency_proof` hook type, LinkedIn only).

**Rule: never state a fact about the agency that is not on this list.** No
invented numbers, client names, awards, revenue figures, staff counts, or
"trusted by" claims. If it is not written below, it does not go in a post. When
in doubt, write a shorter post.

## Verified

- Founded in 1985. Based in New York.
- Represents 180+ acts.
- Has placed acts in 70+ countries.
- Specializes in tribute acts and legacy performers, booked into theatres,
  performing arts centers, casinos, festivals, and corporate events.
- Booking contact: info@loveproductions.com.

## How to use these

- Pick one or two facts per post. Listing all of them reads like a brochure.
- Lead with whichever fact is most useful to a talent buyer, usually the roster
  size or the years in business.
- Follow the length and tone rules in [LinkedIn](../platforms/linkedin.md).
